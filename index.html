Reads your recent activities to draw the card. Nothing is stored on a server.

Strava only lets a few athletes connect to a new app until it has been reviewed. If connecting fails, export the activity as a GPX file and use the Activity file tab.

Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

How do I get a GPX or FIT file?
  • Strava (on a computer): open the activity, click ⋯ on the left, then Export GPX.
  • Strava app: you can't export from the phone app; open strava.com in your phone's browser and use the steps above.
  • Garmin Connect: open the activity, click the gear icon, then Export to GPX or Export Original (a FIT file).
  • Wahoo, Coros, Huawei, Komoot…: look for Export or Share → GPX on the activity.
